Sound Design & Mix

Sound that carries the picture.

Bespoke sound effects, music mastering, and stem mixing that make a piece feel finished and keep viewers watching.

Start a project See what's included

What's included

  • Bespoke SFX layering

    Custom effects built in layers rather than dropped in from a library.

  • Music mastering

    Tracks balanced and polished to sit properly under picture.

  • Stem mixing

    Dialogue, music, and effects mixed as separate stems for flexibility.

  • Delivery loudness

    Final levels set to the standards of each platform or broadcaster.

How it works

  1. 1
    Spotting

    We decide where sound needs to lead, support, or stay out of the way.

  2. 2
    Design

    Effects and atmospheres built and placed against picture.

  3. 3
    Mix

    Levels, EQ, and dynamics balanced across all stems.

  4. 4
    Master & deliver

    Loudness-checked masters plus stems if you need them.

What you receive

  • Final stereo mix
  • Separate dialogue, music, and effects stems
  • Platform-specific loudness versions
  • Textless or music-free versions on request

Good fit for

  • Editors who need a finishing mix
  • Brands with social content that has to sound premium
  • Creators who want sound design, not just levels
